1. To configure PHP on hosting, we need PHP 8.
2. Unzip the DUMP.sql file to your PC.
3. Create a database (save password and username).
4. Import the database (DUMP.sql) on your hosting.
5. Upload archive files to your hosting at the root of the site.
6. Open the settings.php file.
7. At the end of the file find the $databases.
8. In the fields 'database', 'username', 'password' enter your data that we recorded in paragraph [5].
9. Create an SSL certificate.
10. Open the website.
